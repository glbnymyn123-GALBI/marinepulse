About MarinePulse

MarinePulse maps and analyses public reports of dolphins, whales, Mediterranean monk seals and dugongs along the coasts of Lebanon, Syria, Gaza and Egypt. Each record was collected from social media, news and citizen-science posts, then reviewed for species, place, date, group size and credibility.

The Analysis tab tests whether each mammal type is reported differently from country to country, and every number on it is recomputed in the browser from the loaded file. The same analysis runs in Python in analysis/marine_mammal_stats.py.

Research
Gal Binyamin, for IMMRAC (Israeli Marine Mammal Research and Assistance Center)
Data
354 reports, 2014-2026
Methods
Chi-square and Monte Carlo exact tests, adjusted residuals, two-proportion z-tests, Welch t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, Kruskal-Wallis, Poisson rates and regression
Built with
Leaflet, hand-written SVG charts, Python (pandas, SciPy, statsmodels)
